Names of Heart Parts

1. Right atrium
Receives oxygen-poor blood.
2. Right ventricle
Pumps blood toward the lungs.
3. Left atrium
Receives oxygen-rich blood.
4. Left ventricle
Pumps blood to the body.
5. Superior vena cava
Returns blood from the upper body.
6. Inferior vena cava
Returns blood from the lower body.
7. Pulmonary arteries
Carry blood to the lungs.
8. Pulmonary veins
Carry blood from lungs to heart.
9. Aorta
Main artery supplying the body.
10. Tricuspid valve
Between right atrium and ventricle.
11. Pulmonary valve
At the exit of the right ventricle.
12. Mitral valve
Between left atrium and ventricle.
13. Aortic valve
At the exit of the left ventricle.
14. Septum
Separates the heart's right and left sides.
15. Coronary arteries
Supply the heart muscle with blood.
16. Myocardium
The heart's muscular wall.

How Blood Circulates

Body → Vena cava → Right atrium → Tricuspid valve → Right ventricle → Pulmonary valve → Pulmonary arteries → Lungs

Lungs → Pulmonary veins → Left atrium → Mitral valve → Left ventricle → Aortic valve → Aorta → Body

Conclusion

The human heart is a four-chambered muscular pump. Its valves keep blood moving in the correct direction, and its two sides work together to send blood to the lungs and the rest of the body.
